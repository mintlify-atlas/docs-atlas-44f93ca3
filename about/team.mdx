EduPets was not built by a company or a research lab — it was designed and shipped entirely by seven high-school students who wanted to prove that meaningful educational software is within reach of any team willing to think carefully about pedagogy and put in the work. What you see running in the browser is the result of their shared curiosity, late-night debugging sessions, and genuine desire to make math less intimidating for the next generation.

## Who we are

The team behind EduPets are seven students from the **media técnica de software** — the technical high-school software development track. They are **Team Six**, and beyond their coursework they share a common passion for video games, music, and programming. That combination is no accident: the game-like structure of EduPets reflects exactly the kind of interactive, rewarding experiences the team members themselves grew up enjoying, now channelled into something that can genuinely help younger students.

## Our mission

> "We believe the future of our generation is technology, and our project aims to demonstrate that software can help us in everyday life."

That conviction sits at the heart of every design decision in EduPets. The team did not set out to build a flashy demo — they set out to build something useful, something a real child would want to open again tomorrow.

## Why we built EduPets

EduPets was created as a student project after the team arrived at a shared observation: for many young children, mathematics can feel complex and tedious, and that difficulty is compounded for students who struggle with attention or have learning differences such as ADHD. Rather than accepting that as inevitable, the team decided to investigate how software could lower the barrier.

The result is a platform that reframes arithmetic practice as an act of care. Learning math doesn't have to feel like a chore — by making every correct answer mean something visible and immediate (a hungry pet gets fed, a tired pet gets to sleep), the team hoped to create intrinsic motivation for daily practice. EduPets is the team's proof-of-concept that educational software can be engaging, fun, and pedagogically meaningful all at once.

## Tech choices

The team made deliberate, pragmatic technology choices suited to a student project that needed to ship quickly, run reliably for free, and remain easy for every team member to understand and contribute to.

- **Python + FastAPI** — Python's gentle learning curve and FastAPI's minimal boilerplate let the team build a working backend quickly, without getting bogged down in framework conventions.
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Rather than introducing a frontend framework, the team chose plain JS to keep the codebase accessible to all contributors and avoid dependency overhead on a project where every page load counts.
- **Vercel** — Free tier deployment with automatic previews on every push made Vercel the obvious choice for a student team that needed a public URL without managing servers or paying hosting bills.
- **localStorage** — Persisting pet state and scores directly in the browser eliminated the need for a database entirely, keeping the architecture simple and deployments stateless.
